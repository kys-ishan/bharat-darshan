Taj Mahal

Start the 360° tour Full detail page
Agra, Uttar Pradesh · 1632–1653

Marble, light and geometry

The Taj Mahal is not one building. It is a composition: a domed mausoleum, a mosque, a guest house, four minarets and a long garden, all set on a raised platform beside the Yamuna. Every axis is deliberate — the pool down the middle doubles the dome, and the four minarets lean very slightly outward so they fall away from the roof if they ever fall.
