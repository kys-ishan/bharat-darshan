Where the pictures come from

Real photographs

Every image is one of the licensed Wikimedia Commons photographs used on the map, zoomed into a detail.

No account

Rounds are generated in your browser from the local data file. Nothing is uploaded and no score is stored anywhere.

No leaderboard

There is no ranking to climb and nothing to sign up for. Your score disappears when you close the tab.
